OUR STORY & MISSION

BORN FROM CAR CULTURE & STREET PASSION

Brown Boys Customs (BBC) began with a straightforward vision — to bring world-class, bespoke automotive luxury and customization builds to the Greater Toronto Area. Operating out of Unit 1C30 inside Westwood Mall, Mississauga, our goal has become the go-to destination for car enthusiasts, transport drivers, and performance builders alike.

What started as meticulous experimentation with Rolls-Royce style fiber-optic headliners quickly grew into a full-scale customization hub. We recognized that vehicle owners in Ontario wanted custom work done right — without sloppy wiring, without damaged trim, and with an eye for clean, refined details.

Equally central to our identity is our deep connection to Punjabi and South-Asian trucking culture. From authentic hand-made decals, emblems, and pillar candles to custom bumper stickers, license plate frames, and collectable cast models, BBC proudly bridges international custom styling with hometown heritage.
