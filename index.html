ABOUT BROWN BOYS CUSTOMS

WHO WE ARE

Brown Boys Customs started at Westwood Square with a simple vision — to bring world-class, bespoke automotive customization to the Greater Toronto Area. We specialize in lighting, dashboard builds, custom decals, truck accessories and more. Every build is handled in-house with precision, passion and attention to detail.
