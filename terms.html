2. Custom Orders, In-Shop Installations & Deposits

Due to the specialized, bespoke nature of custom automotive work:

  • • Custom Steering Wheels & Tailored Carbon Fiber: Manufactured according to exact customer specifications and vehicle fitment. A deposit is required prior to starting production and is non-refundable once raw fabrication begins.
